# P0-06 fix-2 (review pass 2 of 3): whitelist enforcement behind Velocity

## Result
Paper enforces the whitelist. Its refusal comes after Login Success, during the configuration phase (the normal 26.1 ordering), not before it. No config change was needed (case a of the brief). Both directions are proven below: a non-whitelisted `probe_bot` is refused with the message, and after `whitelist add probe_bot` it joins.

Wording note: Paper's refusal text is "You are not whitelisted on this server!" (no hyphen). The brief's "not white-listed" was a paraphrase.

## Probe change
`compose/probe/login-probe.mjs` now continues past Login Success when `CONTINUE=1`: it sends Login Acknowledged, answers Known Packs with an empty list, answers keep-alive and ping, finishes the configuration phase, and holds in play for `HOLD_MS`. A configuration disconnect prints its reason as ASCII. `IDLE_MS` sets the no-data timeout (default 15 s). Without `CONTINUE`, the probe behaves as before.

## Evidence, non-whitelisted (whitelist empty, `white-list=true`, `enforce-whitelist=true`)
Command: `docker run --rm -e CONTINUE=1 -e HOLD_MS=8000 -e IDLE_MS=30000 --network geldar-phase0_default -v <probe dir>:/probe:ro node:24-alpine node /probe/login-probe.mjs velocity 25565 velocity`

[01:43:44 INFO]: Disconnecting probe_bot (/<redacted address>:38492): You are not whitelisted on this server!

An earlier attempt right after startup produced no answer within the idle timeout (Paper logged nothing, Velocity showed only the connect). A retry got Login Success at once. This was a one-off startup delay, not a whitelist effect.
