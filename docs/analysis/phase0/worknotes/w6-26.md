(`wave6/.geldar/adam/`), read against `wave6-main` (ade2e2b).

## Why the Mind did not wake on damage

1. `golem/src/body/wake-conditions.ts:44` raises `unexpected_damage` only when
   `this.wants('unexpected_damage')`. `wants` checks the running intent's `wake_on`
   (`wake-conditions.ts:37-39`). With no intent running there is no `WakeConditions` watching
   health, so no damage wake is ever sent. The file's own header says "Anything not listed in
   `wake_on` never wakes the Mind from here" (`wake-conditions.ts:17`).
2. The Mind ignores `damage` events by design. `animus/src/attention/controller.ts:184-186`:
   "Actions, plan results, reflexes and damage reach the Mind through the Body's own wakes."
   So a `damage` event with no Body wake behind it changes nothing.
3. The `panic_burrow` reflex was abandoned (no suitable block). An abandoned reflex does not
   raise `reflex_danger`. Only the executor's `reflex_danger` path sets the wake
   (`golem/src/body/executor.ts:74`), so a reflex that gives up leaves the Mind unwoken.

Spec: 5.3.1 says the Body stops and asks the controller when an action fails or a `wake_on`
condition fires (docs/spec.md:1683). The spec does not say the Body must wake on damage while no
intent is running. Item 2 closes that gap on the Mind side (see the decision record).

## Why the idle wake stopped

The idle watchdog works. It re-wakes exactly every 60 s, which is `idleWatchdogMs = 60_000`
(`controller.ts:105`). It is armed in `thoughtSettled` when nothing is in flight and no intent is
active (`controller.ts:145`), and it fires `addCandidate('idle')` after the period (`controller.ts:243-251`).

The 16:25:16.12 thought settled, so the watchdog was due about 16:26:16. The death arrived at
16:26:14. `death` calls `clearState()` (`controller.ts:169-170`), which sets `ended = true`
(`controller.ts:382`), stops all timers (`controller.ts:390`), and makes `armIdle` return early
(`controller.ts:245`). The Mind is quiet for the rest of this life.

The brief's note that "no idle timer re-woke it" is therefore explained by the death, not by a
gate, cooldown, backoff, or dedupe. No suppression was recorded for it, and `coalesced_in_flight`
never applied because `inFlight` was false after each settle.

## Why death did not wake it

`death` is not a wake reason. It is not in `BODY_WAKE_REASONS`
(`animus/src/attention/policy.ts:15-24`), and the controller handles it by ending the life
(`controller.ts:169-170`). The spec says death is permanent and a new life has a distinct life ID
(docs/spec.md:1579 and :1418). Waking the Mind after death would start a thought for a life that
no longer exists. So the quiet after death is correct. The run then sat idle until the harness
aborted it.

## Conclusion

- Not a hang. `thoughtStarted` and `thoughtSettled` are paired (`thinking-loop.ts:117`, `:148`).
- Damage did not wake the Mind because no intent was running, so no `wake_on` watched health.
- Idle stopped because the death, 58 s after the last idle thought, cancelled the 60 s watchdog
  and set `ended`.
- Death does not wake the Mind, as the spec intends.

## Decision taken (item 2)

`docs/decisions/0065-damage-wakes-the-mind.md`: a `damage` event from the Body becomes an
`unexpected_damage` candidate in the Mind's own controller, coalesced through the existing pending
set and spacing, so a burst makes one wake. Death keeps ending the life. Idle is unchanged.
