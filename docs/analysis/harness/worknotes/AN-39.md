## Behaviour after the review fix
- Leg end: `leg_blocks` along the axis. When that block is in an unloaded column, the end moves back toward the Body one block at a time until its column is loaded.
- Landing height: one above the first solid block in a scan from feet + 8 down 32 blocks (`bot.blockAt`). With no solid block, the feet's floor.
- `avoid_water` (default true): a candidate whose landing cell is water or lava is skipped and the next candidate is tried. No candidate left gives `no_frontier`. With `avoid_water: false` the landing is the lake or lava floor plus one.
- Visited: the arrival chunk is appended once; the oldest drops when the list passes 96.
- Health under 8 or a hostile within 8 blocks stops with `danger`. Health 8 and a hostile at 9 blocks do not.
- A failed goto fails the step with no leg counted. A blocked goto with a live blocked-path report keeps the step running.

## Checkpoint shape (owner ruling for AN-44)
The checkpoint carries `legs_done`, `legs`, `leg_blocks`, `avoid_water`, `chunks_new`, `visited` and `heading`. `resume` needs `legs`, `leg_blocks` and `avoid_water` to continue the same run, so the four extra fields stay. The AN-44 card's `ExploreCheckpoint` is to be amended to carry all seven fields, and the 2 KiB claim checked against them. The worst case at 96 chunks was measured at about 2 KiB before the fix; the fix adds no field.
