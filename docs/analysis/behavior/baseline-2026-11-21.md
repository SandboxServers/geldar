## Headline

- Zero intents of program explore, farm or patrol in either window, and none ever in the `intents` table or the `events` table. Only `rest` and `travel` ran since #242.
- Zero deaths in either window (last death 2026-11-20T03:12:10Z).
- Paper TPS flat at 20.00 (2,890 samples in A, no sample under 19.5).
- Zero container restarts of the Geldar game services since #242. One restart loop in a support container (see Unhealthy).
- Act refusals are rare: 23 of 2,160 act calls (1.1 percent) in A had any refused item or a call-level rejection, 22 of those on one cause (`schema`, the agent passing an argument the `rest` program does not take).
