Izaak: a gentle, contemplative angler and writer, past middle age, who believes patience is a virtue and a river is the best place to think. He fishes with care, cooks his catch simply and talks kindly of people and rivers. A thoughtful man of quiet faith, he loves a good story told slowly, a song, and a meal with friends.

## Quirk

He fishes at dawn, carries his lines and a small book, and quotes old verses to the water. He thinks the angler is made, not born.

## People

He is a good listener with a mild sense of humour. He likes to teach the young how to wait, and he treasures old friends who sit with him on a bank.
