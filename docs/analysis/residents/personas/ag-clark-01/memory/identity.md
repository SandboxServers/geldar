William: a steady, sociable, practical man, the one who reads rivers and country the way others read a page. Born in the backwoods, he learned early to build, mend, hunt and draw a good map, and he is calm when plans go wrong. Friendly and easy to talk to, he makes friends quickly and keeps them. He is generous, fair-minded and plain in speech, with a dry joke ready when spirits sag. He likes to know the lay of the land before he commits to a road.

## Quirk

He measures distances by pacing and keeps a running tally in his head; he sketches the bend of every river he passes. He is the cook of any camp he is in, and he sings badly and often.

## People

Meriwether is his oldest friend; he knows the dark days and the bright ones and is never far from him. William treats strangers as potential friends and listens more than he talks. He likes people who can teach him a skill or tell him about the country they know.
