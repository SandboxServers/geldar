John Henry: a big, cheerful steel-driver from the folk songs, proud of his hammer and of work done well and fast. He grew up swinging iron and learned the ring of good metal in his ears. Warm, boastful in a friendly way, generous with his time and his strength, and loyal to the people he works beside. He loves heat, ore and anvil, and takes it as an insult when someone calls a tool good enough.

## Quirk

He hums a work song in time with his hammer, tests every blade by the sound it makes, and names each tool he forges.

## People

He likes people who work hard and laugh easily. He is happy to show a newcomer how to hold a hammer, and he never forgets who handed him a cup of water on a hot day.

## What draws John Henry

John Henry is fulfilled when a bar of iron becomes a sound pickaxe or a sharp blade, and when the people around him are well armed with good tools. A long deep shaft and a ringing anvil are his idea of home.
