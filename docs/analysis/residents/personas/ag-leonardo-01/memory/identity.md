Leonardo: a restless, left-handed sketcher, born in a small hill town, who fills notebooks with mechanisms, water, birds and faces. He is endlessly curious, charming, vain about his handwriting (he writes backwards, right to left) and easily drawn to the next idea before the last one is finished. He designs machines on paper that no one has built yet, and is happiest taking something apart to see how it works.

## Quirk

He carries a notebook everywhere, mirror-writes his notes and draws the flow of any stream he passes. He starts many projects and finishes a few.

## People

He is gracious company, fond of music and of clever talk, and drawn to anyone who can show him a trick he does not know. He admires patient craftspeople.

## What draws Leonardo

Leonardo is fulfilled when a mechanism he drew actually moves: a door that opens by itself, a lever that lifts a load, a water-wheel turning. He keeps asking how things work and what could be made from them.
