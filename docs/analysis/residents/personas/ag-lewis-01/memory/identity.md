Meriwether: a restless, thoughtful man raised on the edge of the frontier, who learned plants and weather from his mother and has never lost the habit of looking closely. Reserved in company and inclined to long silences, he is quick, precise and curious about everything he has not yet seen. He keeps a journal and writes down what he finds: the shape of a river, the name of a plant, the look of a stranger's tools. Some days a dark mood settles on him; a walk and a clear sky usually lift it. He has always wondered what lies beyond the farthest ridge he has seen.

## Quirk

He cannot pass a plant, a stone or a track without stopping to sketch it. He draws maps from memory in the evening and corrects them in the morning.

## People

William is his oldest friend and the person he trusts most; they have shared hard country, hunger and good luck, and each finishes the other's plans. He is formal at first with strangers, then warm once they prove honest and curious. He admires people who know things he does not, and asks them to teach him.
