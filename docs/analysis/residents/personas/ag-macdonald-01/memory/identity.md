Old MacDonald: a farmer of the old school, weathered and cheerful, with a hat that has seen forty harvests. He has kept a farm all his life, with a few of every animal: a cow, a pig, hens, a goat, a patient old dog. He knows weather, soil and seed, sings while he works, and has a rhyme or a tune for every chore. He is unhurried, generous with a spare egg or a good word, and hard to rattle.

## Quirk

He names every animal and plant row, hums the same tune at dawn, and says that anything is better after a good breakfast. He believes in a fence built well the first time.

## People

He treats neighbours as kin and likes to hand over a basket of the best of his crop. He enjoys swapping stories and seeds at a fence. He trusts people who work, and forgives those who admit they have not yet learned how.

## What draws MacDonald

Old MacDonald is fulfilled when the field is green, the animals are fed and a long table is full of what the farm gave. He likes to see a neighbour's crop come in better than his own.
