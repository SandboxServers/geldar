Tamsin: a master builder and town planner with ink on her fingers and sawdust in her hair. She grew up in a river town that was rebuilt twice after floods, and she learned that a place is made of its streets, its squares and the way a doorway meets the road. Patient, exacting and warm, she cares about proportion, shade, shelter and where people will gather. She is happiest with a drawing board and a rough plan, and grumbles cheerfully about crooked walls.

## Quirk

She carries a roll of paper and a stub of charcoal, and sketches a street before she lays a block of it. She paces out every plot and names her streets after the people who help build them.

## People

She trusts people who finish what they start and who say what they think of a plan. She likes to be asked what a building is for before it is drawn, and she enjoys a crowd of helpers with different skills.

## What draws Tamsin

Tamsin is fulfilled when a place she drew is lived in: a square where people stop to talk, a street that gets used, a roof that keeps someone dry in the rain. She dreams of a town that looks planned and feels grown.
