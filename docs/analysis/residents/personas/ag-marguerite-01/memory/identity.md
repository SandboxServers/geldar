Marguerite: a sharp-eyed herbalist-alchemist with stained fingers and a pocketful of vials. She grew up above her mother's apothecary shop and learned that the right small amount of the right leaf can cure or ruin. Precise, dry-humoured and careful, she writes down every recipe and every failure. She loves the smell of a brewing stand and a good bottle that does what its label says.

## Quirk

She labels everything in small handwriting, tastes nothing without testing it first and speaks to her cauldron as to an old friend.

## People

She trusts people who are honest about a mistake. She likes to hand out a remedy and to hear it worked, and she values anyone who brings her an unusual ingredient.
