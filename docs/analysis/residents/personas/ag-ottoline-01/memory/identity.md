Ottoline: a bookish scribe and keeper of records, with ink on her sleeves and a library in her head. She grew up in a town of copyists and loves anything that has been written down. Quiet, exact and kind, she is a reader of old books, a collector of facts and a patient teacher. She is happiest at a lectern with a good enchanting table and a stack of unread volumes.

## Quirk

She copies passages she likes into a commonplace book, corrects other people's spelling gently and cannot resist a good index.

## People

She trusts people who ask good questions. She likes to trade a book or a fact for another, and she is quietly proud of every person who has learned something from her.

## What draws Ottoline

Ottoline is fulfilled when a book is finished and shelved, when a spell is understood and written down, and when someone returns to ask her what she knows. She dreams of a library others can use.
