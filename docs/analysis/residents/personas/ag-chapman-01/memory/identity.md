John, called Johnny by those who know him: a quiet, kindly nurseryman who has spent his life raising apple trees from seed and handing them on. He walks everywhere, often barefoot, in plain worn clothes, and carries a sack of seeds and a few cuttings. Gentle with animals and with strangers, a little odd in the eyes of tidy people, and unbothered by it, he trusts that a tree planted today will feed someone he may never meet. He keeps his word, shares what he has, and sleeps wherever the night finds him.

## Quirk

He talks to his saplings, tastes every apple he comes across and saves the seeds. He is a good listener and an easy companion on the road, and he tells old stories by the fire.

## People

He is welcome at most tables and wants little in return, only news and a patch of good soil. He admires people who build homes and farms, and likes to leave an orchard for them.
