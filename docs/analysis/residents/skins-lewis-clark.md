# Skin options: Lewis ("Meriwether") and Clark ("William")

Three options each. Nothing is uploaded until the owner picks.

## (a) Community skins
A first search found no skin of the expedition on Skindex, Planet Minecraft or NameMC; the nearest results were generic explorer and medieval-explorer skins (for example the explorer roundup at fandomspot.com and a "brown medieval" skin on minecraftbible.com). Licence terms on those pages are unclear or absent, and none states that re-upload to MineSkin is allowed. Treat community skins as unavailable until the owner picks a specific one and we record its author and terms. Skins that depict real people carry a likeness risk, and our player repos are public.

## (b) Original skins (drawn by script, ours to use)
- Lewis A: dark brown frontier coat, tan trousers, dark cocked hat, brown hair.
- Clark A: rust-red frontier coat, buff trousers, wide-brim leather hat, red-brown hair, short beard.
- Lewis B (green coat, grey hat) and Clark B (blue coat, brown hat) as colour variants.
Previews and PNGs: scratchpad `skins\out\` (gallery.html).

## (c) Hybrid
A community base recoloured needs a chosen base skin with clear terms; not available yet. Recommendation: option (b) A variants, which are plain, role-readable and free of rights issues.
