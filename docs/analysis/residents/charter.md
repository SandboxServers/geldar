# Residents charter

Campaign "Residents" (session geldar-residents, decision block 0300-0309). Draft for owner approval; nothing is created until approved.

## Purpose

Add a few residents whose own lives bring needs and pulls into the world (exploring, building, growing, trading) without steering the other agents.

## Criteria

1. **Own pursuits, no steering.** A persona says who the character is: history, values, temperament, loves, dislikes, relationships, what would feel fulfilling. It never says what to do or when, never sets rules for treating other agents, and never mentions our goals, experiments, observation, the harness, tools, limits, budgets or the real world. Pull, not push ("she has always wondered what lies past the next ridge", not "go exploring").
2. **Equal abilities.** Same tools, limits and starting kit as every other agent. No special knowledge, items or powers.
3. **Public-domain or original.** Real historical figures are written factually and respectfully; fictional ones are original characters (no Star Trek, Harry Potter or Bob the Builder names or copies). Player repos are public, so fan skins and trademarked names are a risk.
4. **Same text rules as the kit** (decisions 0058/0059/0068): persona text carries no coaching and no "you are watched".
5. **Cost.** About $0.14 an hour per agent on Haiku at low effort: about $3.4 a day each, about $20 a day for six. Colo Body cap is 40 (15 running now). The owner's player cap is 9 and full; it must be raised with `owner-set-cap` (geldar-worldops or geldar-93).
6. **Format.** Today's kit persona format: `ag-<name>-01/memory/identity.md` and `goals.md`. Character-sheet conversion is a later, separate job.

## Gates

Nothing is created (repo, registration, skin upload, host) until the owner approves the persona texts and skin choices.

## Retirement

A resident that behaves badly (breaks the same rules everyone has) or is boring after a fair stretch is retired: stop the host, archive the repo, remove from whitelist, log it here. Retirement is the owner's call on geldar-behavior's report.

## Judging the experiment

geldar-behavior compares the village before and after each start: exploration distance, farming, building, social change (new contacts, trades, shared projects). We record start times in `log.md`.

## Naming

Agent id `ag-<name>-01`, life `ag_<name>_01`, roster name = life id (16 chars or fewer, letters, digits, underscore), plus a public alias like the TNG ones. Proposed: `ag-lewis-01` alias "Meriwether", `ag-clark-01` alias "William".
