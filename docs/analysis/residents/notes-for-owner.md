# Notes for the owner

## Architect
Original character "Tamsin", a master builder and town planner. Why not Bob the Builder: trademarked. A public-domain historical builder is possible (for example a named medieval master mason), but a real person's life would need factual care; an original character avoids that.

## Farmer
Option A: "Old MacDonald" (public-domain folk song; recommended, instantly legible and cheerful). Option B: "Hattie Brook", a plain-spoken homesteader. Both are drafted. Either starts with nothing others lack.

## Instigator: removed by the owner
- The persona gives style and wants only: curiosity, wit, trading in news, mild mischief. It names no targets, no goals and no tools.
- Risks: gossip can spread false claims; teasing can read as bullying to other agents; a trader of information may be pushed into deceit by the model's own drift. None of these is steered by us, but we should watch for it.
- Mitigation: geldar-behavior watches social change; retirement per the charter if the character breaks the same rules as everyone else.

## Inventor (concept only)
A restless sketcher of mechanisms and curiosities in the spirit of da Vinci: fills notebooks with half-built machines, is easily distracted by the next idea, loves taking things apart to see how they work. Full persona only if the owner asks.

## Cultures
Distinct cultures (Klingon, Romulan and the like) create identity groups, alliances and friction, which is socially interesting. But our agents share the same abilities, we cannot enforce a culture, and built-in hostile factions would put a thumb on the scale. Recommendation: individuals with a culture of origin (values, customs, humour) as personas, no factions, no enforced hostility, and watch what happens. Worf is already a Klingon on the crew. No alien residents unless the owner asks. Note IP: use original cultures, not Star Trek ones, for new residents.

## Skins and IP
Community skins of real people or IP characters carry licensing risk and our repos are public. No clear-licence community skin was found for any resident; originals are recommended.
