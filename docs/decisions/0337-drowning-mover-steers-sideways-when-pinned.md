# 0337: the drowning mover steers sideways when it is pinned

- Status: Decided (Body survival fix after the ag_billy_01 drowning). Part two (the earlier start, `DROWNING_START_AIR` 12) is decided by the owner (geldar-93, "B in a measured form") and amends 0194; part one of geldar-93 (the deep-water travel cost) is not in this record's commit and stays open.
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Cites: 0194 (swimming is a Body movement with an air budget; `hazard_escape` starts at air 12 (this record) and its 40-tick quiet window and 200-tick give-up), 0184 (the reflex and the goal end), 0336 (the boxed-in unstick, unchanged here).
- Why: ag_billy_01 (BlueRanger) drowned at 17:17:14Z on 2026-11-15 at (-268, 60.2, 197.5). The hazard_escape run started at air 8 (y 55.6, six blocks under the surface). The drowning mover, `swimUp`, held jump only and never steered. Every hurt row for the nine seconds to death carries the same position and y 60.2, so the Body rose to a cap and stayed there while its air ran from 8 to 0. The run gave up at the 200-tick limit with air 0 and a second run was still in the water at death. Read-only diagnosis; the block data is not logged, so the cap shape is a hypothesis (a roof over a column whose exit is sideways).
- What: the drowning mover gets a second mode. Every 20 ticks it compares the Body's height with its height at the window start. When the Body gained less than 0.3 blocks in 20 game ticks (the window is counted with the reflex's tick clock, not loop turns), it searches outward through water and air (a breadth-first search, at most 8 steps) for a breathable cell: head level swimmable and the cell above it open air, so a dry pocket or a lake surface both count. A solid block is a wall, so the route goes round it. It keeps the last target while the nearest is less than 2 steps nearer (ties and the first search go east, south, west, north), and swims the route with jump held, aiming at the next cell. Each later window re-picks; a window that gains 0.3 blocks or more drops the sideways target and the mover goes back to the old behaviour. When an undercut-bank column is also in reach, the mover takes whichever of the column or the lateral route has fewer blocks to swim (equal goes to the column). The 200-tick budget and the 120-tick swim share do not change.
- Logs (operator lines only, no agent text): the `start` line of every hazard_escape run now names the mover (`swim_up` for drowning, `walk_out` otherwise), the names of the three blocks over the head and the two under the feet, and the nearest open column (direction and distance). When the sideways fallback engages, one `lateral` line with `event: engage` names the direction, the distance and the height gained in the window; each later change of target writes another `lateral` line with `event: retarget`, so a run has one engage line and one line per change.
- Earlier start (owner decision, geldar-93): the reflex trigger is `DROWNING_START_AIR` = 12 (was the literal 8, the old `DROWNING_AIR`). The trigger check, the "recovered" test (air above 12) and the `hazard_escape` run use it. The quiet window (40 ticks) and the 200-tick give-up do not change.
- Amendment to 0194: 0194 a and b said the reflex stays at air 8. That rule is replaced by 12 for the reflex. The swim controller keeps its own hand-over line, `SWIM_HAND_OVER_AIR` = 8, so its hold behaviour does not change.
- Constant relations (air, 20 is full): `DROWNING_START_AIR` 12 = `SURFACE_AIR` 12 > `ABORT_AIR` 10 > `SWIM_HAND_OVER_AIR` 8. A deliberate swim surfaces at 12, the same tick the reflex fires. So the swim abort at 10 never runs while the reflex is live: the reflex starts at 12 first and cancels the action. Swim is OFF for goto now, so nothing in production hits this. `SWIM_HAND_OVER_AIR` is below the reflex line and never runs first either; it stays only so the swim hold is unchanged.
