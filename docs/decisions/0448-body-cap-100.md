# 0448. Running-Body cap raised from 50 to 100 in one step

- Status: Accepted (the owner's instruction, 2026-11-27)
- Date: 2026-11-27
- Spec: none. This record changes one number set by decision 0261 (the running-Body cap) as amended by 0447.
- Amends: 0261 item 3d (the running-Body cap, as raised by 0447) and item 3e (the alert point, as set by 0447 item 2). Amends 0447 items 1 and 2 only. Its other rules stand, and 0447 is not edited.

## Decision

1. **The running-Body cap goes from 50 to 100 in one step.** The owner instructed this on 2026-11-27. The staged re-measurement that 0261 item 3e asks for before a raise is skipped on the owner's authority. The 15 sovereign hosts still count toward the cap, as 0261 item 3d says.
2. **The alert point is 80 running Bodies.** The helper writes its one alert line when the running count first reaches 80. This is a chosen margin of 20 below the cap, not a figure the owner set. The code value is `CAP_ALERT_AT` in `body-helper/src/service.ts`, and the default cap is `DEFAULT_GLOBAL_BODY_CAP` in `body-helper/src/config.ts`. `GELDAR_GLOBAL_BODY_CAP` still overrides the default, and the helper still never raises the cap itself.
3. **Go-live adds 50 players in one step, after the first 31 (0447).** The per-owner player cap does not change. It stays at 50 (decision 0052, migration 0002), so the 50 new players are split across two owners: 5 under one owner and 45 under the other. No schema or token-tool change is needed.
4. **Measure after go-live.** TPS and MSPT are measured after the change, not before it. The bar is the one 0261 item 3e sets: TPS 20 and MSPT under 25, read from the Paper telemetry about 30 minutes after the change.
5. **Rollback.** If the measurement is bad, stop the newest players with `player-stop`, one per player. The cap can also be lowered by setting `GELDAR_GLOBAL_BODY_CAP` in the helper's environment and restarting the helper.

## Control status

The helper unit is not installed on the Colo node today. Until it is, the cap of 100 is a policy and a code default only: nothing on the node enforces it. The control in place is the measurement in item 4, read after go-live, and the rollback in item 5.

## Consequences

- The shard can hold 100 running Bodies. The Paper server (`MAX_PLAYERS` 400 in `compose/colo.yml`) and the Colo sizing were not benchmarked with more than one Body (`docs/operations/capacity-and-cost.md`). The owner accepts that risk for this step, as 0447 records.
- Decision 0261 and decision 0447 are not edited. This record is the amendment, and the README row says so.
