# 0341: sleep skips obstructed beds, remembers refusals, and caps each walk

- Status: Decided
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Cites: 0338 (free beds first, the time-limit timeout), 0190 (the sleep hard stop), 0225 (the bed key of a generic refusal), 0226 (the bed census).
- Why: after 0338 the sleep success rate did not change (about 33% of night sleeps). The 30 s budget went to beds refused as obstructed (60 of 86 bed tries in the diagnosis), to walks cut by the time limit, and to beds with no path, the same few beds every night, at about 7 to 10 s per bed.
- What (1), obstructed beds are not walked to: before the walk, the Body checks the two cells above the bed, the one above the head and the one above the foot. A bed is obstructed only when both are full solid cubes that suffocate: `boundingBox` `block`, one full-cube shape, and not transparent. Slabs, stairs, lanterns, glass, leaves, trapdoors, carpets, unloaded cells and cells whose shape cannot be read do not obstruct, so the real attempt decides. Mineflayer's own bed check (4.39) has no obstruction test. The rule is a conservative reading of the server's suffocating rule, which the Body does not vendor: a bed with free cells is never skipped, and a bed with one blocked cell is walked to and the server's refusal decides. Entities are not counted: a monster in the bed box is left to the server's monsters refusal (0190, 0225), and an agent standing on a bed does not obstruct it.
- What (2), refusals are remembered: a bed refused as obstructed or with no path (`NO_PATH_TEXT`) is remembered for the same Body for 10 minutes (`REFUSAL_MEMORY_MS`). A remembered bed ranks last, after the free beds and the occupied beds. The memory is per Body (keyed by the bot object) and is not shared between Bodies. An entry is dropped once it is older than 10 minutes.
- What (3), a per-bed walk cap: one walk to a bed is cut after the larger of 7 s and 0.45 s per block of straight-line distance from the Body (wall ms at 1x, scaled by `timeScale`); a bed 28 blocks away gets 12.6 s. A walk cut by the cap fails that bed as `timeout` with `the walk to the bed took too long`, and the loop tries the next bed. The 35 s hard stop (0190) and the 30 s budget (0338) are unchanged, and the hard stop still cuts a walk in progress.
- What (4), a bed the Body already stands beside is tried first with no walk: a free bed within one block of the Body on each axis goes ahead of the other free beds and is sleeping-tried without `moveNear`.
- What (5), the outcome when every walk is cut: when every bed the search tried had its walk cut by the cap, the search ends `timeout` with `every walk timed out`. Any other all-tried search with no sleep stays `blocked`, as 0338 states.
- Not in this record: an optional bed target on the `sleep` verb; a contract, MCP or prompt change; any change to the `timeout` reason of the time limit. No world change. `docs/spec.md` is not touched.
- Rollback: revert the commit. The change touches `golem/src/actions/sleep*.ts` and their tests, and this record; no state, contract or config changes.
- Tests: `sleep-obstructed.test.ts` pins the two-cell skip for all four facings and both halves, the non-suffocating cells (glass, leaves, slab, stairs, lantern, trapdoor, carpet, unreadable, unloaded) that do not skip, the beside rule on each axis, the beside-first order, the refusal memory (ranked last, per Body, kept inside 10 minutes, dropped after, and for a no-path refusal), the distance-scaled cap (a bed 28 blocks away is reached), the all-cut timeout, and the per-bed cap at 7 s. `sleep-hardstop.test.ts` expects the all-cut `timeout`. Revert checks are listed in the commit's report.
