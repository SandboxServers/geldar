# Ideas

Where scope creep goes. An idea that isn't in [the spec](spec.md) or the current packet is written down here instead of being built. Each idea is a line or a short paragraph: what, why it might matter, and which phase could take it. Ideas move into the spec (and then into a packet) by a deliberate decision, never by being half-built in a PR.

| Idea | Why it might matter | Earliest phase |
|---|---|---|
| Shadowed parameter in the identity registry log: `RegistryClient.java:236` declares `Bucket bucket`, which shadows the field `String bucket`, so `:245` logs `Watching bucket` with an object (`RegistryClient$1@<hash>`), not the bucket name. The skins client logs one such line too. | Ops reads the log line as a readiness signal; a fixed line would show the bucket name. It is a one-line production change, so it waits for a plugin card. | Phase 1 (next plugin change) |
| Kino thought ticker: the agent's latest public thought as a caption strip under its monitor. | Fits the Back to the Future II wall of screens, but puts location and thought on one page; needs a privacy look under decision 0138. Left out of decision 0166 (assumption A5). | Phase 1 (Kino) |
| Kino with more than 4 monitors (a 3x3 wall). | The Back to the Future II wall shows more screens; the cap of 4 is an owner ruling (decision 0075) enforced by the relay, and the feed load has to be measured first. | After the Kino load budget |
| Maven mirror or caching proxy for CI runners. | Maven Central HTTP 429s keep failing the plugin and neoforge jobs (main push run 44209178309 on 2026-11-17/18, earlier merge-queue ejections); the retry-once step in ci.yml is a mitigation. A mirror or proxy is a larger infrastructure change; the owner approves PRs, not infrastructure, so it is parked. | Unscheduled (needs an owner decision) |
| Absorption ray: unconfirmed hypothesis that endpoints exactly on a block boundary read the touching cells in one direction only (MH-2 review F-B). No asymmetry was found in 640,000 probed pairs (MH-2 verification); the guarantee is stated for interior endpoints only. | If the hypothesis holds, a /tp to whole-number coordinates puts an eye on a boundary, and floor() then favours the cell above, so the muffled tier can differ by direction. Confirm before any fix; fix only if /tp to integer coordinates matters for muffled hearing. | Phase 1 (MH-2 follow-up) |
| Rename the Body from golem to soma (package `@geldar/golem`, the `golem/` directory and the names built on it). | "golem" is a Minecraft term (iron and snow golems), so it is confusing next to the game's own vocabulary; the Body is the agent's body and "soma" says so. Cost: every sheet card (W3-W8, W9-W12, the NeoForge twins) and many docs and tests name paths under `golem/src/`; the package name, Dockerfiles, compose, `geldar.ps1`, CI and the kit repo (mirror and cards) reference it. It would have to land when no cards are in flight, or all card paths would be rewritten together; until the operator decides, the sheet cards keep using today's `golem/` paths. "soma" has no hits in the repo as of 2026-11-18 (searched all tracked files, including Dockerfiles and compose; the only substring match is `somaxconn` in a sysctl test; the kit repo mirror was not searched). Unconfirmed: the operator recalls that the owner mentioned splitting the Body off before merging something, or having the NeoForge Body replace golem; no note, card or decision in the repo says so. Not started. | Not in the sheet campaign; after a quiet point with no cards in flight |
| Hand of god and serendipity: rare owner-discretion intervention in an agent's life (a nudge or an act of nature) for extreme situations. | The owner has used it at his own discretion in extreme situations and says it keeps immersion intact. Not built in v1 or v2 (Paper is robot-only and autonomous; NeoForge humans reach agents only through game chat); applied more judiciously in v3. This is a deferral, not a prohibition; the invisible-hand and nudge wording in the NeoForge notes stays pending operator revision. | v3 |
| Glass tabs: a bottom tab bar (Glass, Kino, Map, Roster) with a Roster tab (the agents from `/api/agents` in alive and dead groups, a profile pane with an agent's latest entries on a wide screen), a Map tab (embed or link BlueMap) and Kino inside the same shell. | The mobile design handoff sketches them (decision 0410, A1). Map and Kino are separate pages with their own policies today; thoughts must not sit next to locations without a privacy look (the Kino thought ticker row above). | After the Glass redesign ships |
