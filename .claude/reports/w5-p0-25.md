## Behaviour notes and deviations

- `ThinkerHub` (`hub.ts`): `ws` server on `mind.thinker.host:port`, path `/v1/thinker/stream`, `maxPayload` 1 MiB; identity `local-<n>` assigned at accept; welcome names `mind.thinker.name` with `accepted_models` = offered intersected with the pin keys; at most 8 connections (extra closed 1013); a connection carrying an `Origin` header is refused (a web page reaching loopback; the real lantern-local client sends none, the interop test proves it). `pick(model)` takes the least loaded live session with a free slot. `stop()` sends `drain` (reconnect 5 s by default) and closes.
- `ThinkerSession` (`session.ts`): hello within `helloTimeoutMs` (10 s) else close 1008; a second hello closes 1008; frames before hello, binary, non-JSON, oversize, invalid and colo-only frames are dropped and counted; heartbeat older than 45 s means down (`isLive`); `goodbye` stops new jobs; every outbound frame is validated; completion text is also capped at 32 KiB in bytes.
- `JobLedger` (`jobs.ts`): foreign, duplicate and late answers are dropped and counted as anomalies. An answer that crosses our own `cancel` is dropped and counted separately (`crossedCancel`), not as an anomaly (an honest Thinker races a cancel). Admission counts the hub's own open jobs per model against the offered `max_concurrent`; the heartbeat load is stored for diagnostics only (a stale heartbeat would otherwise block slots).
- `LocalThinkerClient` (`local-client.ts`): `no_thinker` at once; deadline already passed gives `timeout` without sending; timeout after deadline plus grace (2 s default) sends `cancel`; abort sends `cancel` and gives `cancelled`; a job that is not a valid frame gives `internal`. `thinkerId` is the welcome name. `response_format` and `reasoning` are sent when the job has them (decision 0049).
- The packet's checks are `./geldar.ps1 check` (TypeScript); the cargo lane does not apply.
- Interop test: `lantern-interop.test.ts` imports the real `LocalThinker` from `lantern-local/src/thinker.ts` by relative path (no import side effects) with a scripted chat function: round trip with schema and `think` passed through, error mapping, cancel aborting the model call, concurrency.
