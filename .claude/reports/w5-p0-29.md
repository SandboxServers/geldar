## Decisions made inside the card

- Quoted section: heard lines, sign text, `blocks_summary`, nearby agents' names and health text, the current plan text, speech in the wake events and the overheard digest, and relationship names and impressions. It is one section headed `QUOTED WORLD TEXT`, closed by `END OF QUOTED WORLD TEXT`, at the end of the user message. Every item is one line `- <kind> {json}` with sorted keys; strings are cut at 512 characters and `<`, `>`, DEL, C1 controls, the soft hyphen, zero-width and bidirectional marks, U+2028 and U+2029 are written as `\uXXXX`.
- Memory-derived text (goals, beliefs, commitments, episodic memories, recent-thought lines) is not in the quoted section: it is the agent's own stored text, rendered as JSON strings one per line inside blocks 5 and 6.
- Event payloads (`events_since_last_thought`) are shown only as type counts.
- Validator order: size, JSON, allow-list on the raw JSON (so an unknown verb is `not_allowed`, not a wall of schema errors), contract schema, bounds. Without constrained output (`constrainedOutput: false`) exactly the two Q5 steps apply (one leading think block, one json fence) and `onNormalise` reports them; the default is constrained, with no normalisation.
- Degraded reflection: the contract needs all six fields, so degraded means `memories_to_keep` of at most 2, the lists `relationship_updates`, `beliefs_add`, `beliefs_remove` and `goals` empty (`not_allowed` otherwise), and `day_summary` kept.
- Argument shapes of the five programs are checked in `bounds.ts` (mirroring `golem/src/body/programs/*.ts`); a model-supplied `checkpoint` is bounded to 4 KiB.
- `say` is the top-level field, not an action; the contract has no `say` verb.
- "Days alive" in block 6 is the world day from the snapshot (no birth day is in the prompt variables).
