Interface request: add `'command'` (a leading `/`) to `SayRefusal` in `senses.ts`. `too_long` is never used: the text is cut to 256, not refused.
- `BotEars` implements only `heard()`; `drainEvents()` belongs to `RecentEvents`. P0-32 composes both into one `Ears`.
- Health drops: no `RecordSink` method carries health, so `RecentEvents.noteHealth(health, tick)` is called by the wiring (P0-32) on a bot `health` event. Interface request: none, but the wiring must call it.
- Intermediate wake, reflex and action digest entries carry `type` (`action_ended`, `intent_ended`, `reflex_fired`, `wake`, `health_drop`) plus ids, state, reason and a detail cut to 200 characters.
- No `chat.display_name` exists in `shards/phase0.yaml` (only `chat.rate_limit_seconds`, `max_chars`); `BotEars` takes an optional `displayName`.
