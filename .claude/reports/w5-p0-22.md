## Notes and deviations

- The heartbeat frame is `type: "heartbeat"` as the schema and Appendix H define (the card says `load`).
- Advertised `max_tokens` is a constant 1024 (Appendix H example); the pins carry no per-model max.
- The 32 KiB output cap is counted in UTF-8 bytes (stricter than the schema's character cap); a test covers multi-byte text.
- A job whose model is unknown or not in `accepted_models` gets `error{model_unavailable}`. A reply for a job that finishes after its connection closed is discarded. A job aborted by `cancel` sends no frame.
- `drain` closes the socket and reconnects after `reconnect_after_seconds` (no backoff); in-flight jobs are aborted on any close.
- The config loader refuses `sha256:RECORD_AT_DISPATCH` unless `allowPlaceholders` is set; the real `shards/phase0.yaml` therefore does not load until the digests are recorded (a test pins this). `main.ts` calls the strict loader.
- `OLLAMA_URL` and `GELDAR_MIND_WS_URL` must be loopback (`127.0.0.1`, `localhost`, `[::1]`).
- Live case `lantern-local/test/ollama.it.test.ts` is written, not run (`GELDAR_IT=1 GELDAR_OLLAMA=1`).
